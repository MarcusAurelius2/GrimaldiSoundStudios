About Marcus Grimaldi

I'm Marcus Grimaldi, a mixing engineer and sound designer. My work includes over 400 million streams, Top 3 Billboard placements, and Nightbirde's “It's OK” which hit #1 on iTunes in five countries.

I've worked alongside Teddy Swims, Ashanti, Sam Tinnesz, and John Splithoff. On the film side, my sound design work has been recognized at SXSW, the Nashville Film Festival, and the Woods Hole Film Festival.
